## 🧭 How I work

- **Security from day one:** cookie-based sessions, OTP and rate limiting; IDOR and XSS fixes; Postgres RLS; role-based access control with auditing.
- **From data model to deploy:** Spring Boot and Next.js APIs, versioned migrations (Flyway, Prisma), and CI/CD with tests and post-deploy smoke tests.
- **Running in production:** Hetzner VPS, automatic deploys from `main`, encrypted backups on Cloudflare R2, and Sentry monitoring.
- **Documented decisions:** every relevant change ships with a spec explaining the *why*, not just the *how*.

## 🏢 Highlights at Axyon (private repositories)

**🍔 Food Delivery Suite**: ordering platform with cart, payments, admin panel and subscriptions.
Closed IDOR and XSS in the payment flow, fixed refunds on mismatched charges, and built encrypted
external backups (R2) plus automatic disk cleanup on the VPS.
`Java/Spring` `Next.js` `PostgreSQL` `Docker` `Hetzner`

**📚 Storyverse**: reading app with forum, ranking, gifts and AI chat.
Implemented email-confirmation auth, server-side AI quota (with metrics and caching), role-based
moderation with auditing, SSE presence and lossless offline→cloud sync.
`Spring Boot` `Next.js` `Supabase` `SSE` `Hetzner`

**📈 Orbit CRM** · [demo](https://orbit-crm-jade.vercel.app): multi-tenant CRM with a client portal
and SEO/social media modules.
Blocked self-promotion to super-admin, closed portal leaks, added RLS validated in CI with automatic
migrations, and built the SEO audit module with Playwright.
`Next.js` `Supabase (RLS)` `Playwright` `CI/CD`
